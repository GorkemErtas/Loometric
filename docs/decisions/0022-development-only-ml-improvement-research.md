# ADR 0022: Controlled offline improvement research with spent validation

Status: accepted for M09A, before new actual model comparisons (2026-11-23).

## Context and options

M08C's actual Forest12/Hist31 estimators remain experimental. EXP-006 found stage
failures and hidden-shift vulnerability. Options are tune on repeatedly inspected
validation, consume reserved tests, enlarge v1 arbitrarily, or freeze a bounded
development study. Choose the last. Serving and planning are unchanged.

## Decision

Bind the original ten M08A receipts through the immutable EXP-006 configuration
fingerprint and retained corrected study receipt. Use its development-only reader;
never decode global outcomes/gap/test labels or regenerate actual study data.
Diagnostic entity context may be projected from pre-shift features only and must
never enter an estimator. Rare difficult-event flags are not available from the
authorized label projection; do not reconstruct hidden RNG effects or pretend
individual residuals identify them.

Predeclare configs/ml_improvement_v1.json and commit it before actual comparisons.
Three new expanding folds evaluate original-training date fifths [2/5,3/5),
[3/5,4/5),[4/5,1), each with one preceding observed date purged and strict global
label maturity. Previously evaluated dates/labels are reused development evidence,
not a pristine holdout. No outer validation enters candidate selection.

Compare Grouped Mean, raw Forest12 and raw Hist31 with six fixed improvements:
same trees plus declared planned features; more regularized engineered forest and
histogram; baseline-plus-histogram residuals; partially pooled stage histogram.
Stage models require at least200 training rows/five training dates and weight
n/(n+200), otherwise use global. Numeric threads1, seed42, no early stopping,
no pooling worlds or stress refits. At most300 estimator fits,20,000 rows/fit,
1200seconds checked between fits. This is not a hard OS training watchdog.

Derived features use only planned inputs: worker-minute budget, dimensionless
offered standard-load ratio and one-piece attainment quantum, an explicitly ordinal
skill/complexity interaction, WIP share, planned downtime fraction and stage/load
interactions. No realized values, identities, dates, seed/scenario or censoring
outcome is a predictor. No label/data/prediction clipping or silent imputation.

Raw-tree permutation uses only each held-out training-fold evaluation subset,
up to1000 IDs selected without labels, three deterministic within-start-date
shuffles. Inspect all13 marginal inputs and coherent demand/capacity blocks;
report physically inconsistent marginal perturbation rates. These are predictive
sensitivity probes, not feasible factory interventions or causal importance.
Retrain both raw trees with three fixed feature-block exclusions: clock, downtime,
WIP/release split. Ablations are diagnostics, not additional selectable candidates.

Research designation requires at least5% macro MAE gain over Grouped Mean and2%
over the strongest raw tree, positive gain in every baseline world,2/3 temporal
wins/world,≤5% stage and positive-P95 regressions,≤10% worst-error regression.
Apply all stage/tail cells across the four worlds' pooled nonoverlapping OOF rows.
Use the existing half-development-mean/half-benchmark weighting and fixed1%
simplicity preference. These criteria designate RESEARCH_CANDIDATE only; no model
is promoted. Freeze selection before outer diagnostics. Report old validation and
paired stress results for every declared candidate, including regressions, without
reranking. Original EXP-006 gates/metrics/decision remain immutable.

Use500 date-cluster bootstrap replicates for matched historical MAE differences.
Their descriptive intervals reflect repeated dates, not independent crews/worlds
or factory confidence. Count entity/shift support and show per-seed heterogeneity.
Quantify identical-feature baseline/disruption pairs and their empirical mixture
error lower bound. Feature-only sparse joint-cell support and load extrapolation
are risk diagnostics, never detection claims about unobserved shift. Conditional
OOF residual bands reuse selection folds: no calibration/coverage guarantee.

Publish create-only ignored reports, matched predictions, fit/feature/protocol
provenance, environmental identities and a clearly non-serving benchmark research
artifact. Reproduce deterministic bytes twice. Keep timing/memory separate from
deterministic receipts. V2, if justified, is a design proposal with hypotheses and
point-in-time controls; do not generate it or revise v1.

## Consequences

This study can identify representation deficiencies and uncertainty limits in the
declared simulator. It cannot establish real-world accuracy, independent final
generalization or operational readiness. M01/M08A reserved tests, M08C trusted
registry/artifacts and frozen planning engines remain untouched. No new dependency,
serving change, automatic retraining/promotion, deployment or scheduling work.
